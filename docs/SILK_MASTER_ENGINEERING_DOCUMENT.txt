PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document is the authoritative engineering and architectural blueprint for
SILK. It reflects the current implementation state of the project, documenting 
the physics systems, artificial intelligence routines, and audio-visual juice.

SILK starts in the vertical portrait arena immediately without title screens.

===============================================================================
1. REALIZED PLAY AREA & ALIGNMENT SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================
- Playable Area limits: Left/Right X: [-15.0, 15.0], Floor/Ceiling Y: [1.0, 27.5]
- Vertical Boundaries: Flat solid walls at X = -15.0 and X = 15.0. Measurement 
  unit ticks scroll downwards continuously, creating the endless vertical 
  ascension illusion.
- Static Camera Lock: Fixed perspective camera at (0, 14, -38) looking at 
  (0, 14, 0) with a fixed horizontal FOV mode. This completely locks the render 
  view within the vertical portrait borders.

===============================================================================
2. REALIZED CORE TRAVERSAL & PHYSICS SKELETON
===============================================================================
- SPIDER CEILING SWEEP (WEAVER ANCHOR): 
  Spawned as a giant mechanical sphere (radius = 2.4) patrolling autonomously. 
  The top end of the silk string (tether anchor coordinate) locks dynamically 
  to the Weaver's real-time position.

- PENDULUM SWING & STEERING:
  The player capsule (radius 0.4, height 1.8) is suspended below the Weaver.
  Left/Right input directly steers the pendulum to build momentum in mid-air.

- WALL SLIDE FRICTION & ELONGATION:
  Steering into a wall locks the player's horizontal coordinate. Friction slides 
  the player down, dynamically stretching the base length of the tether up to 
  a maximum breaking point.

- TENSION & FLINGING:
  Releasing the wall while under tension flings the player across the screen 
  (launch power based on accumulated tension). Remaining on the wall at critical 
  tension accumulates strain. If critical strain persists for over 2.6s, the 
  silk snaps, causing a Game Over.

===============================================================================
3. COMBAT & WEAVER AI STATE MACHINE
===============================================================================
Combat resolves strictly through physics impacts and traversal states.

- PLAYER OFFENSE (FLING ATTACKS):
  Player damages the Weaver (35 DMG) by impacting them while in the "LAUNCHING" 
  state with a launch power threshold >= 80%.

- WEAVER OFFENSE (CONTACT DAMAGE):
  Weaver damages the player (1 DMG, 1.2s i-frames) via direct physical overlap 
  while the Weaver is actively in the hostile "DASHING" state. 

- WEAVER STATE MACHINE:
  1. SWEEPING (Red): Dormant patrol. Moves along the ceiling at a fixed velocity.
  2. DASHING (Orange/Red/Cyan): Triggered by taking damage. Enters a Prep phase 
     (0.6s), a high-velocity Thrust phase towards the player's coordinates, and 
     a Recovery phase (0.5s). Loops 2 times.
  3. RETURNING (Gray): Ascends vertically back to the ceiling patrol line.
  4. DEFEATED (Dark): Triggers at 0 HP. Game Win state.

- BERSERK OVERDRIVE (<= 50% HP):
  Sweep patrol speed doubles. Dashing sequence increases from 2 strikes to 3.

===============================================================================
4. AUDIO-VISUAL JUICE & SPECTACLE
===============================================================================
- PROCEDURAL AUDIO (Tone.js):
  - Tension Synthesizer: FM Oscillator paired with a Lowpass Filter and LFO. 
    Frequency, harmonicity, and gain scale dynamically in real-time based on 
    the current silk tension percentage (0.0 to 1.0).
  - Combat Audio: Membrane synth for low-end player damage impacts; Pink noise 
    synth for high-frequency hit-flashes.
  - State Audio: Tension drone modulations scale oscillator harmonics and 
    LFO speeds based on the Weaver's active state (Berserk/Defeated).

- VISUAL JUICE:
  - Screen Shake: Globally broadcast via Event Broker. Amplitudes and durations 
    react to flings, Weaver dashes, damage hits, and silk-snap failures.
  - Procedural Particles: Object-pooled geometric bursts tracking velocity vectors 
    with linear gravity decay, color-coded by event source (Green = Player, 
    Red = Weaver).
  - Emissive Shifting: The player's mesh emissive lighting pulses and shifts 
    from green to red/orange based on active silk tension loads.
