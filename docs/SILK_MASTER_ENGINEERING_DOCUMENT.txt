[AI ASSISTANT GUIDELINES: HAVOK V2 PHYSICS]
When expanding or optimizing physics-driven mechanics, adhere to the Babylon.js V2 Physics API (Havok) best practices:
1. Object Pooling: Prefer separating collision bounds and mass by instantiating a 
   reusable PhysicsShape and binding it to individual PhysicsBody instances. 
   Avoid the PhysicsAggregate wrapper for large pools to save memory.
2. Legacy Code: Ensure no V1 PhysicsImpostor syntax is generated.
3. 2D Constraints: When constraining 3D physics to a 2D plane, rely on static 
   invisible glass walls to natively sandwich the simulation plane rather than 
   manually clamping the Z-axis in the render loop.

1. REALIZED PLAY AREA & ALIGNMENT SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================
- Playable Area limits: Left/Right X: [-15.0, 15.0], Floor/Ceiling Y: [1.0, 27.5]
- Vertical Boundaries: Flat solid PBR walls at X = -15.0 and X = 15.0. Measurement 
  unit ticks scroll downwards continuously, managed by ParallaxScrollSystem, 
  creating the endless vertical ascension illusion.
- 2D Physics Plane: The Z-axis is constrained natively via invisible front and 
  back static Havok collision walls at Z = 3.5 and Z = -3.5.
- Static Camera Lock: Fixed perspective camera at (0, 14, -38) looking at 
  (0, 14, 0) with a fixed horizontal FOV mode. 

===============================================================================
2. REALIZED CORE TRAVERSAL & PHYSICS SKELETON
===============================================================================
- GHOST ENTITIES (KINEMATICS):
  The Player and Weaver move via strict custom trigonometric ECS logic (processed 
  by PlayerKinematicsSystem and WeaverTraversalSystem), but exist in the Havok 
  simulation as ANIMATED (Kinematic) PhysicsBodies. This allows Havok to track 
  their exact hitboxes natively without gravity interfering.

- DECOUPLED PLAYER LOCOMOTION STATE MACHINE:
  Player movement kinematics are modeled using a strict state machine pattern. 
  PlayerKinematicsSystem delegates coordinate updates and velocity checks to 
  isolated classes implementing the IPlayerState interface inside the 
  `src/gameplay/player/states/` directory:
  1. AIRBORNE (PlayerAirborneState): Translates swing inputs, gravity metrics, 
     drag damping, and detects limits.
  2. WALL_SLIDING (PlayerWallSlidingState): Handles friction factors, WallBug 
     attaching coordinates, and passive tension increases.
  3. LAUNCHING (PlayerLaunchingState): Integrates high-speed sling release 
     forces, launch damping, and checks limit boundaries.

- THE TETHER AS A ONE-WAY RATCHET:
  1. Elongation: The maximum length of the rope can only increase while wall sliding.
  2. Instant Reeling: Reeling slack updates maxLength immediately.
  3. Strict Fall Clamp: Momentum converts cleanly into a pendulum arc when arrested.

- TENSION & FLINGING:
  Releasing a wall-slide under tension flings the player. Persisting at critical 
  strain for >2.6s snaps the silk. 

- STRAIN ISOLATION:
  Tether strain calculation, rumble triggers, and structural snap events are 
  isolated entirely in TetherStrainSystem, leaving VerticalBoundarySystem strictly 
  responsible for spatial coordinate clamping against floors and ceilings.

===============================================================================
3. COMBAT & WEAVER AI STATE MACHINE
===============================================================================
- PLAYER OFFENSE & COUNTER-PLAY (FLING ATTACKS & PROJECTILE DESTRUCTION):
  * Fling Attacks: Impacts Weaver while LAUNCHING with >= 80% power.
  * Projectile Smash: The player is invulnerable to web shot projectile damage 
    during a tension-driven fling/launch (LAUNCHING state). Colliding with a web 
    projectile in this state immediately destroys (recycles) the projectile, 
    triggering satisfying impact particle splats and a minor camera shake without 
    inflicting any damage. Standard post-damage iframes allow projectiles to pass 
    through safely.

- COCOON TRAPPING, STRUGGLE & SQUASHED WALL-LOCK MECHANICS:
  * Cocoon Encasement: Projectiles have a default diameter of 1.8 units (twice the 
    player's width). If the player is struck in a vulnerable state, they are encased 
    in the web cocoon mesh.
  * Steering and Reeling Dampening: While trapped, lateral steering force and tether 
    reeling speeds are dampened by 50%.
  * Trapped Wall-Lock: If the trapped player contacts a wall, they automatically 
    stick to it. Voluntary release inputs are ignored; the player remains glued 
    until the web is broken or the line snaps from overloaded tension.
  * Alternate Key Struggles: Escaping requires 5 alternating directional inputs. 
    Repeating the same direction consecutively is ignored. On struggle registration, 
    micro-shiver rumbles and web-splat particles trigger.
  * Break-Free Super Fling: Escaping the web while holding a wall-lock rewards the 
    player with a 1.1x fling impulse multiplier and an extra exhaust trail of sparks.

- DECOUPLED COMBAT RESOLUTION:
  CollisionResolutionSystem is the centralized hub for all physical overlapping. 
  It calculates hitbox-hurtbox combat damage, resolves wall clings, and handles 
  projectile-to-player intersection checks. This frees ProjectileSystem to focus 
  strictly on coordinate calculations, pooling limits, and wall-gash sticking.

- WEAVER STATE MACHINE:
  The state machine is evaluated by WeaverBrainSystem, which delegates runtime 
  updates to isolated single-responsibility state classes:
  1. PATROLLING (WeaverPatrollingState - Silver/Yellow): Dormant patrol on the ceiling. 
     Fires telegraph-based web projectiles.
  2. STRIKING (WeaverStrikingState - Sulfur Yellow/Pink): Triggered by taking damage. 
     Consists of Prep (strobe/shiver), Thrust (high-speed homing dash), and Recover phases.
  3. ASCENDING (WeaverAscendingState - Pink): Ascends vertically back to the ceiling 
     patrol line after strikes are finished.
  4. DEFEATED (WeaverDefeatedState - Saturated Black): Triggers on 0 HP. Commences the 
     dual-layer procedural shatter cinematic.

===============================================================================
4. AUDIO-VISUAL JUICE & SPECTACLE
===============================================================================
- TWO-LAYER PROCEDURAL SHATTER (WeaverShatterSystem):
  On death, the Weaver's procedural mesh is partitioned into a dual-layer 
  physics explosion managed by WeaverShatterSystem:
  1. Outer Shell: 20 flat triangles exploded outward via surface normals.
  2. Inner Core: 10 solid, mathematically sealed wedges.
  Debris physics simulations run custom boundary collisions and size-decay scaling.

- SKELETAL JOINT AND IK SEPARATION:
  All analytical Inverse Kinematics (IK) math and rotational joint updates for 
  the Weaver's legs and WallBug crawl cycles are isolated inside 
  LegJointAnimationSystem. This leaves VisualStateDressingSystem completely free 
  to configure material emissives, scale springs, and body bobs.

- PROCEDURAL AUDIO (Tone.js):
  FM Oscillator paired with Lowpass Filter and LFO. Frequency and harmonicity 
  scale dynamically based on silk tension and Boss states (managed by 
  AudioDirectorSystem and TensionSynthesizer).

- VISUAL JUICE & INTERPOLATION:
  * Screen Shake: Broadcast via Event Broker and handled by CameraSystem.
  * Particle Effects: Managed by JuiceSystem (sparks, dust, splats, and trails).
  * Parallax Scrolling: Decorative ticks scroll under ParallaxScrollSystem control.
  * Render Interpolation: Entity visual node positions, scale, and rotations are 
    interpolated smoothly by EntityInterpolationSystem.
  * Materials: PBR materials with screen-space Bloom mapping and customized 
    displacement/warp shader plugins (WarpMaterialPlugin, ProjectileNoisePlugin).
