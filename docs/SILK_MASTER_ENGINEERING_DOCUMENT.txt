===============================================================================
REALIZED PLAY AREA & ALIGNMENT SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================
- Playable Area limits: Left/Right X: [-15.0, 15.0], Floor/Ceiling Y: [0.0, 28.0]
- Vertical Boundaries: Flat solid walls at X = -15.0 and X = 15.0. No platform 
  ledges jut out. Measurement unit ticks scroll downwards continuously, 
  creating the endless vertical ascension illusion.
- Static Camera Lock: Fixed perspective camera at (0, 14, -38) looking at 
  (0, 14, 0) with a fixed horizontal FOV mode. This completely locks the render 
  view within the vertical portrait borders.

===============================================================================
REALIZED COREtraversal PHYSICS SKELETON
===============================================================================
1. WARDEN CEILING SWEEP (ANCHOR)
   - Spawned as a giant mechanical sphere (radius = 2.4, 3x capsule width) 
     patrolling autonomously back and forth along Y = 26.0 between X = [-12, 12].
   - The top end of the silk string (tether anchor coordinate) locks dynamically 
     to the Warden's real-time position.

2. PENDULUM SWING & STEERING
   - The player capsule (radius 0.4, height 1.8) is suspended below the Warden.
   - Left/Right arrow keys directly steer the pendulum to build momentum.

3. WALL SLIDE FRICTION & ELONGATION
   - Steering into a wall locks the player's horizontal coordinate to prevent bounces.
   - Friction slides the player down, dynamically stretching the maxLength of 
     the tether. 
   - Elongation past base limits generates critical tension load. Remaining on 
     the wall accumulates strain. If critical strain persists for over 1.8s, the 
     rope snaps, causing a Game Over.

===============================================================================
DEFERRED POST-SKELETON IMPLEMENTATION ITEMS
===============================================================================
These systems are pruned from the initial integration pass to guarantee the
absolute stability of the core physical swing, wall-drag, and tension loops. 
They remain documented below as direct blueprints for future development phases.

1. WARDEN EMOTIONAL & ATTACK PHASES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PHASE 1 CYCLE: DORMANT -> HUNTING -> CHARGE_PREP -> CHARGE_ATTACK -> RECOVERY.
- FAKE DEATH STATE: Transition triggers at 0% HP. Emissive lights turn off, 
  audio synthesizers scale down to low-frequency drones, and the Warden is 
  dazed for a 3.0s window.
- PHASE 2 (BERSERK OVERDRIVE): Revives at 50% HP. Visuals switch to a intense
  high-emissive orange glow, movement sweep speeds double, and the audio drone 
  reaches its maximum screeching frequency. Permanent defeat occurs on the 
  next 0% HP threshold.

2. JUICE & VISUAL SPECTACLE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SCREEN SHAKE: Screen positioning translates offset matrices with amplitude/duration
  decays, reacting to collision impacts, charge launches, and state shifts.
- CHROMATIC ABERRATION: Triggered on damage events to split color channels 
  temporarily along horizontal coordinate offsets.
- PROCEDURAL PARTICLES: Burst pools of geometric boxes tracking velocity vectors 
  with linear gravity decay, color-coded by event source (green for player, red 
  for Warden).

3. EXPANDED AUDIO DIRECTOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SPATIAL SFX: Pan coordinates scaled dynamically based on horizontal canvas position 
  relative to the center.
- IMPACT SYNTHS: Membrane synthesizers to deliver low-end kick thuds during player 
  damage events, and pink-noise synthesizers for high-frequency hit-flashes.
- STATE-BASED DRONES: Tension drone modulations that scale oscillator harmonics and 
  low-pass filter Q-factors in sync with the Warden's active state.

4. USER MENU & CONFIGURATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- TITLE SCREEN: Classic cabinet title screen with coordinate-tracked focus indicators.
- VOLUME SLIDERS: Native Web Audio gains mapped to reactive sliders (Master, SFX, Music).
- PERSISTENCE MATRIX: 3 distinct LocalStorage save slots with schema validation check passes 
  for Win/Loss tallies, along with a custom key-rebind matrix.
