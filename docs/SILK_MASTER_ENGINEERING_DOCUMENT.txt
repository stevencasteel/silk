[AI ASSISTANT GUIDELINES: HAVOK V2 PHYSICS]
When expanding or optimizing physics-driven mechanics, adhere to the Babylon.js V2 Physics API (Havok) best practices:
1. Object Pooling: Prefer separating collision bounds and mass by instantiating a 
   reusable PhysicsShape and binding it to individual PhysicsBody instances. 
   Avoid the PhysicsAggregate wrapper for large pools to save memory.
2. Legacy Code: Ensure no V1 PhysicsImpostor syntax is generated.
3. 2D Constraints: When constraining 3D physics to a 2D plane, rely on static 
   invisible glass walls to natively sandwich the simulation plane rather than 
   manually clamping the Z-axis in the render loop.

===============================================================================
1. REALIZED PLAY AREA & ALIGNMENT SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================
- Playable Area limits: Left/Right X: [-15.0, 15.0], Floor/Ceiling Y: [1.0, 27.5]
- Vertical Boundaries: Flat solid PBR walls at X = -15.0 and X = 15.0. Measurement 
  unit ticks scroll downwards continuously, managed by ParallaxScrollSystem, 
  creating the endless vertical ascension illusion.
- 2D Physics Plane: The Z-axis is constrained natively via invisible front and 
  back static Havok collision walls at Z = 3.5 and Z = -3.5.
- Static Camera Lock: Fixed perspective camera at (0, 14, -38) looking at 
  (0, 14, 0) with a fixed horizontal FOV mode. 

===============================================================================
2. REALIZED CORE TRAVERSAL & PHYSICS SKELETON
===============================================================================
- GHOST ENTITIES (KINEMATICS):
  The Player and Weaver move via strict custom trigonometric ECS logic (processed 
  by PlayerKinematicsSystem and WeaverTraversalSystem), but exist in the Havok 
  simulation as ANIMATED (Kinematic) PhysicsBodies. This allows Havok to track 
  their exact hitboxes natively without gravity interfering.

- THE TETHER AS A ONE-WAY RATCHET:
  1. Elongation: The maximum length of the rope can only increase while wall sliding.
  2. Instant Reeling: Reeling slack updates maxLength immediately.
  3. Strict Fall Clamp: Momentum converts cleanly into a pendulum arc when arrested.

- TENSION & FLINGING:
  Releasing a wall-slide under tension flings the player. Persisting at critical 
  strain for >2.6s snaps the silk, causing an immediate Game Over.

===============================================================================
3. COMBAT & WEAVER AI STATE MACHINE
===============================================================================
- PLAYER OFFENSE (FLING ATTACKS):
  Impacts Weaver while LAUNCHING with >= 80% power.
- WEAVER OFFENSE (CONTACT DAMAGE & PROJECTILES):
  Weaver damages player via direct contact while DASHING, or by firing 
  object-pooled kinematic Web Projectiles (managed by ProjectileSystem).

- WEAVER STATE MACHINE:
  The state machine is evaluated by WeaverBrainSystem, which delegates runtime 
  updates to isolated single-responsibility state classes:
  1. SWEEPING (WeaverSweepingState - Red): Dormant patrol. Fires projectiles.
  2. DASHING (WeaverDashingState - Orange/Red/White): Triggered by damage. 
     Prep, Thrust, and Recover sub-phases.
  3. RETURNING (WeaverReturningState - Gray): Ascends back to patrol line.
  4. DEFEATED (WeaverDefeatedState - Dark): Triggers 2-Layer procedural shatter.

===============================================================================
4. AUDIO-VISUAL JUICE & SPECTACLE
===============================================================================
- TWO-LAYER PROCEDURAL SHATTER (WeaverShatterSystem):
  On death, the Weaver's procedural mesh is partitioned into a dual-layer 
  physics explosion managed by WeaverShatterSystem:
  1. Outer Shell: 20 flat triangles exploded outward via surface normals.
  2. Inner Core: 10 solid, mathematically sealed wedges.
  Debris physics simulations run custom boundary collisions and size-decay scaling.

- PROCEDURAL AUDIO (Tone.js):
  FM Oscillator paired with Lowpass Filter and LFO. Frequency and harmonicity 
  scale dynamically based on silk tension and Boss states (managed by 
  AudioDirectorSystem and TensionSynthesizer).

- VISUAL JUICE & INTERPOLATION:
  * Screen Shake: Broadcast via Event Broker and handled by CameraSystem.
  * Particle Effects: Managed by JuiceSystem (sparks, dust, splats, and trails).
  * Parallax Scrolling: Decorative ticks scroll under ParallaxScrollSystem control.
  * Render Interpolation: Entity visual node positions, scale, and rotations are 
    interpolated smoothly by EntityInterpolationSystem.
  * Materials: PBR materials with screen-space Bloom mapping and customized 
    displacement/warp shader plugins (WarpMaterialPlugin, ProjectileNoisePlugin).
