[AI ASSISTANT GUIDELINES: HAVOK V2 PHYSICS]
When expanding or optimizing physics-driven mechanics, adhere to the Babylon.js V2 Physics API (Havok) best practices:
1. Object Pooling: Prefer separating collision bounds and mass by instantiating a 
   reusable PhysicsShape and binding it to individual PhysicsBody instances. 
   Avoid the PhysicsAggregate wrapper for large pools to save memory.
2. Legacy Code: Ensure no V1 PhysicsImpostor syntax is generated.
3. 2D Constraints: When constraining 3D physics to a 2D plane, rely on static 
   invisible glass walls to natively sandwich the simulation plane rather than 
   manually clamping the Z-axis in the render loop.

PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document serves as the authoritative architectural blueprint for SILK. It 
details the underlying logic, structural loops, and systemic relationships 
governing player movement, boss behaviors, and the game loop. SILK bypasses 
introductory screens and immediately initializes within the gameplay loop.

===============================================================================
1. PLAY AREA, ALIGNMENT & STATIC CAMERA SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================
- Playable Area limits: Governed by dynamic bounds configured in the spatial 
  parameters. A continuous vertical scrolling illusion is produced by the 
  ParallaxScrollSystem, moving environmental details downward relative to active velocity.
- 2D Physics Plane: Natively constrained along the Z-axis using static front and 
  back invisible collision bounds. This prevents depth displacement and ensures 
  the physics engine and custom kinematics remain strictly coplanar.
- Camera Position Tracking:
  * Locked State: Camera vertical panning is fully locked while the Weaver resides 
    in its normal patrolling state. Because the player's maximum thread capacity 
    is tethered to the boss, the player naturally suspends within the screen 
    boundaries.
  * Active Tracking State: Panning lock automatically releases once the Weaver 
    initiates an attack or enters a descending state. If the player is forced 
    below a dynamic comfort boundary due to boss movement, the camera pans 
    smoothly to keep both actors in viewport focus.
  * Recovery State: Camera tracks back to static baseline coordinates smoothly 
    once the boss completes its action and returns to patrol altitude.

2. REALIZED CORE TRAVERSAL & PHYSICS SKELETON
===============================================================================
- GHOST ENTITIES (KINEMATICS):
  The Player and Weaver move via trigonometric ECS logic processed by player and 
  weaver kinematic systems, but exist in the physics engine as Animated (Kinematic) 
  bodies. This design permits exact spatial and collision state tracking while 
  releasing the system from passive physics force integration.

- DECOUPLED PLAYER LOCOMOTION STATE MACHINE:
  Player kinematics are isolated into states implementing a shared interface:
  1. AIRBORNE: Manages gravitational descent, swing input steering, and drag 
     damping.
  2. WALL_STICKING: Handles friction coefficients, dynamic wall-clinging 
     offsets, and sliding dampeners. Passive tension charging is restricted 
     exclusively to this state.
  3. LAUNCHING: Governs high-velocity release vectors, target-seeking trajectory, 
     and launch dampening.

- THE TETHER AS A ONE-WAY RATCHET & MANUAL REELING:
  * Elongation: Maximum allowed rope capacity only increases while sliding or 
    clinging to walls.
  * Slack Recovery: Reeling actions immediately update active rope bounds, 
    reducing the slack threshold.
  * Pendulum Constraint: Player movement is restricted to a spherical arc 
    whenever the distance to the anchor exceeds current maximum length, cleanly 
    transferring linear velocity into swing momentum.
  * Manual Reel-In: Input intent maps directly to tether contraction, allowing 
    the player to manually shorten maximum length to climb objects or pull 
    themselves upward.

- PHYSICS-BASED ELASTIC TENSION:
  * Elastic Tautness: Tension is a direct calculation of stretch (the ratio 
    of current length to max allowed length). Tension begins building once 
    tautness passes a specific safety ratio.
  * Progress-Linked Tension Cap: The maximum achievable tension is capped 
    proportionally to the current unreeled rope length. At minimal unreeled length, 
    tension is capped at a low safety stage to prevent immediate, accidental snaps.
  * Tension Release Zones (Fling Stages):
    - Stage 1 (Slack Zone): Safe release. Detaching from the wall results in a 
      standard airborne falling trajectory with a gentle outward nudge.
    - Stage 2 (Taut Zone): Fling launch. Launches player toward the active Weaver 
      anchor point at high velocity.
    - Stage 3 (Overloaded Zone): High-intensity fling. Only available when the 
      line is unreeled near maximum capacity, releasing extreme kinetic power.

3. COMBAT, DYNAMIC WEAVER REELING & SHOCKWAVE RECOVERY
===============================================================================
- MELEE SHOCKWAVE REEL-IN:
  * When caught in the Weaver's shockwave blast, the player's thread is 
    instantly reeled back to its compact initial length, making the player highly 
    vulnerable.

- WEAVER STRIKE SEQUENCE & RETRACTIVE REEL-IN:
  * Damaging the Weaver triggers a structured strike state (Telegraph -> Thrust -> Recovery).
  * On transition to the Recovery phase, the Weaver contracts the player's 
    maximum tether length by a fixed percentage, pulling them closer to danger.
  * At the start of Recovery, the Weaver launches a direct web projectile 
    targeting the player's coordinates.
  * While ascending back to patrol altitude, the Weaver continuously reels 
    the player's maximum thread capacity back to initial starting values.

===============================================================================
4. TENSION UI BAR & COLOR GRAMMAR
===============================================================================
- DUAL-PURPOSE TENSION BAR:
  * Reeled-Out Progress: The fill ratio of the physical bar corresponds directly 
    to the active capacity of unreeled tether relative to absolute system limits.
  * Elastic Tautness Glow: The bar's color and pulse speed reflect active elastic 
    tension stages:
    - Stage 1 (Slack): Neutral color, no glow.
    - Stage 2 (Taut): Alert color, warm glow, indicators of fling readiness.
    - Stage 3 (Overloaded): High-frequency alert, extreme pulsing glow, warning of 
      tether fatigue or imminent snap.

===============================================================================
5. THE FLYING HEALTH BUG SYSTEM & COLLISION KINETICS
===============================================================================
- DYNAMIC SPATIAL LIFECYCLE:
  * Flying units spawn below the viewport limits and ascend vertically. 
  * Upon entering the comfort viewport zone, they pause for a randomized period 
    before continuing their ascent out of bounds.

- RE-CALIBRATED INTERACTION STAGES:
  * Stage 0 & 1 (Slack Overlaps): Harmless. The player pushes or slides past 
    the bug safely without damage.
  * Stage 2 (Active Fling): High-momentum impact. Bounces the bug away 
    unless directed into hazards, which pops the bug and inflicts heavy damage 
    on the Weaver.
  * Stage 3 (Overloaded Tension): Absolute kinetic rupture. Pops standard bugs 
    on impact, releasing homing heal particles that seek player slots on the HUD, 
    or triggers high-velocity wall-bouncing pinball kinetics.
