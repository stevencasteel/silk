1. SYSTEMS OVERVIEW & STACK SPECIFICATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Silk is an arcade-style, physics-driven vertical climber. The player 
is permanently tethered to an aggressive boss character (the Weaver) inside a 
narrow, vertically scrolling elevator shaft. Tension generated by the tether 
is the player's primary means of locomotion and combat.

       [CEILING] (Weaver Patrol altitude: Y = 34)
          |
     O====#====O   <-- Weaver (Patrols horizontally, fires projectiles)
          \
           \       <-- Dynamic Elastic Tether (Length: 5.4m to 38.0m)
            \
             *     <-- Player (Swings, clings, slings from walls)
            / \
[LEFT WALL]     [RIGHT WALL] (X = +/- 14.2)

Dependencies:
- Frontend Framework: React 19
- Language: TypeScript 6
- State Management: Zustand 5
- 3D Rendering: Babylon.js 7
- Physics Engine: Havok Physics V2
- Audio Engine: Tone.js 15
- VFX: Canvas Confetti


2. ECS ARCHITECTURE & Priority Phases
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The engine uses a custom Entity Component System (ECS) to decouple entity state 
from execution systems. Execution is structured into an 8-phase pipeline in 
the update loop of SystemManager.ts:

Phase Index | Enum Value | Core Systems | Primary Operations
------------+------------+--------------+-----------------------------------
0           | Input      | PlayerInput  | Polling mouse, key, touch states.
1           | Intents    | WeaverBrain  | Action request state evaluation.
2           | Kinematics | PlayerKinem  | Swing & pendulum adjustments.
3           | Collision  | VertBoundary | Floor/ceiling & strain check.
4           | Physics    | HavokPhysics | Step Havok, sync animated bodies.
5           | Gameplay   | CombatSystem | Overlaps & damage requests.
6           | RenderSync | CameraSystem | Lerp camera, align visual nodes.
7           | PostRender | ProfilePers  | Save game stats to local storage.

Initialization is scheduled in four distinct steps:
- InitPhase.Bootstrap (0): Base configuration and engine setup.
- InitPhase.World (1): Geometry loading, lighting, and camera pipelines.
- InitPhase.Gameplay (2): Player, Weaver, and minion spawning.
- InitPhase.UI (3): HUD mounting, audio samplers, and input bindings.


3. COPLANAR PHYSICS SANDWICHING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To simulate a clean 2D layout within a 3D physics engine, Project Silk utilizes
Coplanar Physics Sandwiching in HavokPhysicsSystem.ts. 

Instead of manual position clamping every frame, the simulation is sandwiched 
between two static, invisible box boundaries parallel to the XY plane:

[ BACK WALL ]   Z = +3.5  (Static Box, friction: 0.1, restitution: 0.5)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  SIMULATION PLANE (All active kinematic actors are centered at Z = 0)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
[ FRONT WALL ]  Z = -3.5  (Static Box, friction: 0.1, restitution: 0.5)

This structurally limits physical motion to the 2D plane natively within the 
physics solver, preventing z-axis drift during explosive force impacts.


4. TRAVERSAL KINEMATICS & PLAYER STATE MACHINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The player's movement coordinates are managed inside PlayerKinematicsSystem.ts. 
Player states map directly to separate state logic classes:

                  +-------------------+
                  |     AIRBORNE      |
                  +---------+---------+
                            |
           Wall collision & | Input release /
           Pressing into wall | Fling launch
                            v
                  +---------+---------+
                  |   WALL_STICKING   |
                  +---------+---------+
                            |
           Fling transition | Timer expiry /
           with tension >= 0.427 | Launch completed
                            v
                  +---------+---------+
                  |    LAUNCHING      |
                  +-------------------+

1. AIRBORNE State:
   - Integrates gravity (Gy = -24.0), lateral swing steering force (36.0m/s^2), 
     and drag damping (0.99).
   - Corrects coordinates using a pendulum solver when target distance exceeds 
     the active tether boundary (maxLength).

2. WALL_STICKING State:
   - Clings to walls and slides downward (speed clamped to -9.0m/s).
   - This is the only state where the player can accumulate elastic tension.

3. LAUNCHING State:
   - Scales down gravity (Gy * 0.22) to allow exact, high-velocity target-seeking 
     trajectories toward the stinger tip.

Tether Slack & Reeling:
- No Auto-Slack: The maximum length (maxLength) does not contract automatically. 
  Players retain whatever slack they have earned during maneuvers.
- Manual Reel-In: Pressed via Up (W / Up Arrow). Contracts the tether's maximum 
  capacity by -16.0m/s to climb objects or pull the player upward.
- Elastic Tension: Calculated once currentLength exceeds the tautness 
  threshold (85% of maxLength):
  Tautness = currentLength / maxLength
  Tension = clamp(0.0, maxAchievable, (Tautness - 0.85) / 0.15)
- Length Capping: Caps maximum possible tension based on unreeled rope length to 
  prevent immediate, accidental snaps when reeled close to the Weaver.

Tension Release Zones (Fling Stages):
- Stage 1 (Slack Zone, Tension < 0.427): Standard release. Standard falling 
  trajectory with a gentle outward push (5.5m/s).
- Stage 2 (Taut Zone, Tension >= 0.427 to < 0.8): Warm alert glow. Launches 
  the player towards the Weaver's stinger tip at high velocity.
- Stage 3 (Overloaded Zone, Tension >= 0.8): Extreme-power fling. Chromatic 
  aberration effects, hitstop delays, and massive kinetic release.

Fatigue & Snap-Back Physics:
If the player exceeds maximum tension limits (1.0), TetherStrainSystem.ts logs 
a damage strike (up to 3 maximum). 

To prevent instant death, the system triggers a Violent Snap-Back:
1. The player is forced off the wall into AIRBORNE with a 0.5s recoil stun.
2. The allowed thread capacity is immediately cut in half (maxLength * 0.5).
3. The player is whipped toward the stinger tip at high velocity (snap force: 55.0).
4. The third strike snaps the thread completely, causing instant death.


5. BOSS COMBAT & WEAVER STATE MACHINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Weaver's AI (WeaverBrainSystem.ts) utilizes five operational states:

            +-------------------+
            |    PATROLLING     | <-------------------------+
            +---------+---------+                           |
                      |                                     |
    Damaged / Bounced | Return ceiling                      | Arrived at
    by player         | condition                           | ceiling Y
                      v                                     |
            +---------+---------+   Strike recovery /       |
            |     STRIKING      | +---------------------+   |
            +---------+---------+ | Contract tether &   |   |
                      |           | Fire web projectile |   |
             Weaver   |           v                     |   |
             bounced  |     +-----+-------------+       |   |
                      |     |     SHOCKWAVE     |       |   |
                      |     +-----+-------------+       |   |
                      v           |                     |   |
            +---------+---------+ | Blast complete      |   |
            |    ASCENDING      | <---------------------+---+
            +---------+---------+

1. PATROLLING State:
   - Moves back and forth along the ceiling at Y = 34.0.
   - Fires target web projectiles, telegraphing with a yellow body glow 
     (1.4s delay) before launching the projectile at 15.0m/s.

2. STRIKING State (Strike Sequence):
   - Prep: Squashes body (Y = 0.82, X = 1.15), shivers, and pulls back.
   - Thrust: Lunges toward target coordinates (28.0m/s normal, 36.0m/s berserk).
   - Recovery: Body stretches on impact. Contracts player's maximum thread limit 
     by 25% and fires a target web projectile.

3. SHOCKWAVE State (Counter Blast):
   - Triggered when the player hits the Weaver during normal patrol.
   - Telegraphs for 0.85s, then releases a circular shockwave (radius: 6.8).
   - Inflicts 1 HP damage on impact, triggers knockback, and reels player's 
     thread back to the starting baseline of 5.4m.

4. ASCENDING State:
   - Weaver climbs back to ceiling altitude (Y = 34.0) at a rate of 12.0m/s.
   - Continuously reels the player's thread back to the starting baseline of 5.4m.

5. DEFEATED State:
   - Body scale is reduced to 0.2, legs collapse, and the boss drops out of play.


6. MINION HAZARDS & SPAWNING LIFE CYCLES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minion hazards spawn dynamically based on the Weaver's health to control the 
difficulty curve:

- Weaver HP <= 100% (HP <= 200): Spike Bug Spawning Active.
- Weaver HP <= 80% (HP <= 160): Wall Bug Spawning Active.
- Weaver HP <= 60% (HP <= 120): Health Bug Spawning Active.

1. Flying Health Bugs:
   - Spawns below camera viewport, flies upward, and pauses for 3s to 6s upon 
     entering the dynamic comfort zone before continuing out of bounds.
   - Interactions:
     * Standard Overlap: Harmless. The player slides past or clings to the bug.
     * Active Fling (Stage 2): Shoves the bug. If directed into wall hazards, 
       the bug pops, dealing 15 damage to the Weaver.
     * Overloaded Fling (Stage 3): Instant kinetic rupture. Pops the bug and 
       releases 8 homing green particles that seek the HUD to restore 1 HP.

2. Wall Bugs & Spike Bugs:
   - Wall Bugs: Crawl down walls. Feature spiked hazard zones on Left, Right, or 
     Neither side.
   - Spike Bugs: Crawl up walls. Spiked on the side facing the play area, and 
     feature a spiked head.
   - Defensive Interaction: Projectiles hitting spiked sides disarm the hazards, 
     making the bugs safe to slide or cling on.


7. USER INTERFACE & COLOR GRAMMAR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The UI mimics an arcade cabinet, utilizing progress bars and color indicators 
to convey elastic tension states:

       [ 1 ]              [ 2 ]              [ 3 ]        [ d ]
[===================||=================||==============||====]
  SLACK (0.0 - 0.42)   TAUT (0.42 - 0.8)  FATIGUE (0.8+)   SNAP

- Reel-Out Progress (Width): Filled based on unreeled tether relative to limits.
- Elastic Tautness Glow (Color):
  * Stage 1 (Slack, < 42.7%): Neutral Green. Safe release.
  * Stage 2 (Taut, >= 42.7% to < 80%): Yellow. Fling launch ready.
  * Stage 3 (Overloaded, >= 80%): Red. Rapid pulse. Imminent snap warning.
